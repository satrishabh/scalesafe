This statement applies to scalesafe.co.in and to the ScaleSafe platform (client portal, admin console and the investor pages that our clients send to their investors), operated by ScaleSafe Compliance Solutions Private Limited.

1. Our commitment

We want everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, magnification, voice control or captions, to be able to use our website and platform independently. Many of our clients are SEBI-regulated entities that must make their own digital platforms accessible, and the pages we provide to their investors are part of that obligation.

3. Conformance status

The website and platform are partially conformant with WCAG 2.2 Level AA (and so with WCAG 2.1 Level AA): we know of no failures on the website pages we have tested, but not every check has been done by hand yet, so we do not claim full conformance. This statement is based on our own review (section 9). We will commission an independent audit by an IAAP-certified professional and publish its date and results here.

On 17 November 2026 every page of scalesafe.co.in (home, Solutions, Insights listing and briefings, legal pages and the Accredited Investor Desk) passed our automated WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 Level A and AA checks at 320, 390 and 1366 pixels wide, together with scripted keyboard checks.

4. Known limitations

  • Linked regulatory documents. Circulars and orders we link to are published by SEBI and other bodies. Some are scanned PDFs without tags, which we cannot fix; our briefings summarise their content in accessible HTML.
  • Screen readers. Keyboard access, focus and contrast are tested; a full manual pass with screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack) of the navigation menu and briefing filters is still to be done.
  • Security checks. Our forms use no puzzles or challenges. If a form does not work for you, write to us and we will help another way.

5. The ScaleSafe platform and SEBI-regulated clients

The investor pages that our clients send (completing details from a secure link and e-signing documents) are designed to WCAG 2.1 Level AA: every field has a visible label, errors are announced to screen readers, touch targets are at least 44 pixels, progress is saved automatically, and motion is reduced when your device asks for it.

For clients that must report under SEBI's digital accessibility circulars, we can share our latest accessibility review and support their accessibility audit of the investor journeys we provide. Ask your ScaleSafe contact.

6. Accessibility features

  • A "Skip to content" link on every page, a main landmark, and a logical heading structure (one main heading per page).
  • Pages work with keyboard only; a clearly visible focus ring shows where you are, and focused items are not hidden under the sticky header.
  • Pages reflow on small screens (checked at 320 pixels wide, equivalent to 400% zoom) without horizontal scrolling.
  • On phones, a Menu button opens every section of the site; it works with the keyboard and closes with Escape.
  • Text and controls meet WCAG contrast requirements, and buttons and links are large enough to tap.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Status messages (saved, submitted, errors) are announced to assistive technology.
  • Animations stop when your device has "reduce motion" switched on.

8. If you are not satisfied

  • India: you may approach the Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities or the State Commissioner under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016. Investors of a SEBI-regulated entity may also use SEBI's SCORES platform for complaints against that entity.
  • European Union: you may contact the market surveillance or enforcement authority for the European Accessibility Act in your country.

9. How we test and review

Before each release we run automated checks (axe-core with the WCAG 2.0, 2.1 and 2.2 Level A and AA rules) on every page at 320, 390 and 1366 pixels wide, plus scripted keyboard checks: a skip link and main landmark on every page, no horizontal scrolling, a visible focus indicator on every focusable element, and focus not hidden by the sticky header. We also check by hand with keyboard only and at 200% zoom. Automated tools find only part of the possible issues, so screen-reader testing and the independent audit (section 3) complete the picture.

We review this statement at least once a year and whenever we make a significant change. This statement was last reviewed on 17 November 2026.
