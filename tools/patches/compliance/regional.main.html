This supplement adds to our Privacy Notice for people in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and the United States. Where this supplement and the Privacy Notice differ for you, this supplement applies.

1. About this supplement

ScaleSafe Compliance Solutions Private Limited, Thane, India, is responsible for the personal data described in the Privacy Notice. We serve SEBI-regulated businesses in India; we include these sections because people outside India visit our website and use our platform.

4. Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, without you having to do anything else. Because we never sell or share personal information, nothing changes in how we handle your data, but the page will confirm that your signal was honored.

5. Data we process for our clients

When a client (for example, a SEBI-registered firm) uses ScaleSafe to onboard its investors, the client decides how that data is used. ScaleSafe processes it only on the client's instructions, as its processor (GDPR) or service provider (CCPA). Please send requests about that data to the client; if you send them to us, we will pass them on and help the client respond.
