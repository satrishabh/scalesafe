1. Summary

  • We collect only what we need to respond to you, run our services and keep the Website secure.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.
  • Our Website analytics are cookieless and aggregated.
  • You can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate another person, or raise a grievance at any time, using the request instructions or by writing to privacy@scalesafe.co.in.
  • Please do not send us passwords, Aadhaar or PAN copies, bank or demat account details, or your clients' records through the Website forms or by unsolicited email.

2. The law we follow

We process personal data in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 (the "SPDI Rules"), which apply today, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the "DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 (the "DPDP Rules"), as their provisions come into force. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025; the provisions on notice, consent, security safeguards, breach intimation and Data Principal rights come into force on 13 May 2027. We already apply those standards to the processing described in this Notice.

Under the DPDP Act, ScaleSafe is the Data Fiduciary for the personal data described in this Notice.

Data on the ScaleSafe platform. When a SEBI-registered intermediary or other organisation (our "client") uses the ScaleSafe platform or our professional services, the personal data that the client uploads or makes available to us (for example, details of its employees, directors, investors or clients) is processed by us on the client's behalf and on its instructions. For that data the client is the Data Fiduciary and ScaleSafe acts as its Data Processor under the client agreement. Requests about that data should be made to the relevant client; if you send such a request to us, we will pass it to the client and assist it as our agreement requires.

5. Consent and how to withdraw it

Where we rely on consent, we ask for it through a clear, separate, unticked checkbox that describes the specific purpose. The enquiry form has two separate choices: one needed to respond to your enquiry, and one optional choice for marketing communications. Saying no to marketing does not affect our response to your enquiry.

You may withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawing will be as easy as giving it:

  • use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email;
  • submit a Withdraw consent request through the request instructions; or
  • email privacy@scalesafe.co.in.

Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. After withdrawal we stop the processing that relied on consent, within a reasonable time, unless the law requires or permits us to continue (for example, to keep a record that you withdrew).

7. Transfers outside India

Some of our providers operate global infrastructure, so personal data may be processed outside India. We transfer personal data outside India only as permitted by section 16 of the DPDP Act and any restriction notified by the Central Government, and in compliance with Rule 7 of the SPDI Rules, with contractual safeguards that ensure at least the same level of protection that we apply.

9. How we protect personal data

We implement reasonable security practices and procedures appropriate to the nature of the data, including:

  • encrypted connections (HTTPS with HSTS) for the Website and platform;
  • encryption of stored data provided by our hosting and email providers;
  • role-based access on a need-to-know basis, multi-factor authentication on administrative accounts, and access logging;
  • bot and spam protection on forms, and validation of form input on our servers;
  • written confidentiality obligations for our team and providers; and
  • an incident response procedure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will report cyber security incidents to CERT-In within six hours of noticing them, as its directions require, and, once the relevant DPDP provisions are in force, inform the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals without delay, in the form and within the time the DPDP Rules require. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect personal data and to respond promptly if something goes wrong.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  1. Access: give you a summary of the personal data we process about you, the processing activities, and the identities of others with whom we have shared it and the categories shared.
  2. Correct, complete or update inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal data.
  3. Erase personal data that is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was collected, unless we must retain it by law.
  4. Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent.
  5. Nominate another individual to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable.
  6. Raise a grievance about how we handle your personal data.

You may also review and correct the information you gave us, as the SPDI Rules provide.

How to make a request. Use the Data Principal request instructions or email privacy@scalesafe.co.in from the email address you used with us. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity, and we will not disclose personal data until we have verified it.

Timelines. We acknowledge requests within 2 working days and resolve them within 30 days of receipt. If we need longer for a complex request, we will tell you why and when to expect a response; in no case will we take longer than the maximum period permitted by law.

Escalation. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once the relevant provisions are in force, after first using our grievance process.

You also have duties under section 15 of the DPDP Act, including not impersonating another person, not suppressing material information, and not filing false or frivolous grievances.
