This Notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used on scalesafe.co.in by ScaleSafe Compliance Solutions Private Limited. It forms part of our Privacy Notice.

1. Our approach

  • We use only technologies that are strictly necessary to deliver the Website securely, or to remember a choice you make on it.
  • Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) are cookieless: they do not set cookies, do not use browser storage, do not fingerprint your device and report only aggregated statistics.
  • We do not use advertising, retargeting, social-media tracking or cross-site cookies.

Because we use only strictly necessary technologies, we do not show a consent banner. If we ever add an optional technology, it will stay switched off until you opt in through a consent banner, and this Notice will be updated first.

2. What we use

Name Type Set by Purpose Duration
__cf_bm Cookie Cloudflare (on scalesafe.co.in) Distinguishes people from automated bots to protect the Website; set only when bot protection is triggered 30 minutes
cf_clearance Cookie Cloudflare (on scalesafe.co.in) Records that your browser has passed a security challenge, so you are not challenged again Up to 30 minutes, or as configured for the challenge
Cloudflare Turnstile Script and challenge frame (challenges.cloudflare.com) Cloudflare Checks that a form is submitted by a person rather than a bot, without a puzzle in most cases For the session of the form
scalesafe_privacy_prefs Local storage ScaleSafe Remembers your choices in the privacy controls on this Website, such as hiding the storage notice; stays in your browser and is not sent to us Until you clear it, or use Clear my choices

3. Managing cookies and storage

You can delete or block cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Blocking the strictly necessary items above may stop forms or security checks from working. You can clear the ScaleSafe local storage item at any time with the Clear my choices button on our Privacy Notice page.

4. Contact

Questions about this Notice: privacy@scalesafe.co.in, or write to the Grievance Officer named in our Privacy Notice.
